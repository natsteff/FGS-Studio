Sheet structure

Sections read left to right, then top to bottom. Section arrows move one section; row arrows move the whole row. A section cannot cross a full-width row without moving that row.

Create print sheet

This places finished-size copies on Letter or A4. Direct Download PDF makes one card-sized page without cut guides and may be clipped by an ordinary printer.

Only for Half Page on matching paper, with a printer that supports edge-to-edge printing. Unavailable for card sizes.

In the printer dialog, choose the paper and orientation shown above, print at Actual size / 100%, and turn off Fit to page. Check your printer's printable margins.
